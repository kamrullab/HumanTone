# Personal Message Examples

This file shows how personal messages can sound more natural, direct, and conversational.

The goal is to match the user's actual tone instead of making every message sound overly formal or AI-generated.

---

## Example 1: Checking In

### Generic AI Style

> I hope you are doing well. I just wanted to check in and see how everything has been going with you recently.

### More Natural

> কিরে, কেমন আছিস? অনেকদিন কথা হয় না.

## Example 2: Asking for Help

## Guidelines

When writing personal messages:

- Match the relationship and level of familiarity.
- Do not sound unnecessarily formal.
- Keep messages as short or long as the situation requires.
- Use casual phrasing when appropriate.
- Do not add unnecessary greetings or closing lines.
- Avoid generic AI-style politeness.
- Mix Bangla and English naturally when it matches the user's style.
- Do not force slang, emojis, or informal language.

## Remember

Personal messages should feel personal.

The best message is not the most polished one. It is the one that sounds natural for the person sending it and appropriate for the person receiving it.
