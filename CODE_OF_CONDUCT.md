# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

HumanTone is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

We want contributors and community members to feel comfortable sharing ideas, feedback, and contributions regardless of their experience level or background.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different ideas and perspectives.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with new contributors.
- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Assume good intent when possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or discriminatory language.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Insulting someone because of their writing style, language ability, or experience level.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Feedback and Discussions

Disagreements are normal in open-source projects.

When you disagree:

- Focus on the idea or contribution.
- Explain your reasoning clearly.
- Avoid personal arguments.
- Stay open to different perspectives.

The goal is to improve the project, not to win an argument.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through the appropriate GitHub channels.

Reports will be handled respectfully and with reasonable confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or other content that violates this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the situation, repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions from participating in the project.

## Final Note

HumanTone is built around the idea that people communicate differently.

Please respect different writing styles, language abilities, experiences, and perspectives.

Let's keep the community constructive, welcoming, and useful for everyone.
